Scared of the word "CrossFit"?

It's a group class with a coach. That's it.

Forget everything the internet told you. Here is what actually matters on your first day.

01

It's coached, start to finish

A certified coach runs every class. You are never handed a barbell and left to figure it out.

02

Everything scales

Every single movement has an easier version. Grandparents and college athletes do the same workout, just at different dials.

03

Zero experience needed

Most of our members had never touched a barbell before their first class. That is normal here, not the exception.

04

Nobody is judging you

The loudest cheers go to whoever finishes last. That is not a slogan, it is just how the room works.
