Our whole mission, one hour at a time

The best hour
of your day.

“Get fit, make it the best hour of your day, stay safe, turn up the music, high five some people.” That’s the actual mission statement. Here’s how a class plays out.

:00

Minute 00: The whiteboard

Walk in, drop your keys, and a coach talks the whole room through today’s plan. You don’t need to know what any of the words mean yet. That’s their job.

:10

Minute 10: The warm-up

Music goes up and everyone moves together. Bikes, rowers, stretching. Bad dance moves are optional but encouraged.

:25

Minute 25: Strength

Barbells, dumbbells, or skill work, with a coach watching your form the whole time. Every weight scales, down to an empty bar. Nobody starts heavy.

:40

Minute 40: The workout

The sweaty, fun part. Usually 8 to 20 minutes where the playlist earns its paycheck. Finish first or finish last, the room cheers either way.

:55

Minute 55: High fives

Fist bumps, a group photo if somebody remembers, and a weirdly great mood for the rest of the day. See you tomorrow.

:00 The whiteboard
Scared of the word “CrossFit”?

It’s a group class with a coach. That’s it.

Forget everything the internet told you. Here’s what actually matters on day one.

01

Coached, start to finish

A certified coach runs every class. You will never be handed a barbell and left to figure it out.

02

Everything scales

Every movement has an easier version. Grandparents and college athletes do the same workout, just at different dials.

03

Zero experience needed

Plenty of people walk in having never touched a barbell. Here that’s normal, not the exception.

04

Nobody is judging you

The loudest cheers go to whoever finishes last. That’s not a slogan, it’s just how the room works.
