The practical stuff

Everything you need to know, in one scroll.

Book the night before

Classes are coached, so we cap them. Grab your spot in PushPress the evening before, or same morning if you're an early riser. If a class shows full, text us and we'll try to squeeze you in.

What to bring

Training shoes, water, and clothes you can move in. That's it. We have the equipment, the chalk, and the playlist covered.

Where to park

Right out front at 170 Serenoa Rd, off US-98 in Santa Rosa Beach. Free, easy, and you will not need to parallel park anything.

Come 10 minutes early

Say hi, sign the waiver, tell the coach what you're used to. They'll scale the workout to your level, whether you CrossFit five days a week or haven't in a year.

Any level, honestly

Our classes hold college athletes and total beginners in the same hour. Vacation is a terrible reason to skip a workout and a great excuse to try a new gym.

Bring the crew

Traveling with your gym friends or a fit family? Book everyone in. Group photo with the wave logo afterward is basically mandatory.
