How it works

Four steps. One is the beach.

Buy a pass

Checkout opens right here and takes about a minute.

Reserve your spot

Spots open 24 hours before class. You must be signed up before you walk in, because we fill up and can’t take walk-ins.

Join the group

The whole hour is coach-led: warm-up, mobility, then the workout. Tell your coach you’re visiting and they’ll take care of you.

Go to the beach

You earned it. Workouts post the night before at 5pm if you want a sneak peek.

Trouble signing up? Please text 850-502-3454 instead of just showing up, since we can’t guarantee a spot at the door.

Want to do your own thing?

Book open gym instead.

Group classes are a team effort: we move through the whole hour together. If you’d rather follow your own program, reserve an open gym slot. A coach is in the building, the equipment is all yours, and you don’t take a spot from someone in class.

Drop-in questions

Quick answers.

I’ve never done CrossFit. Can I still drop in?

Yes. Tell your coach when you get there and they’ll scale everything for you. You won’t be the first person to try CrossFit on vacation.

Does the month pass renew?

No. The 1-month unlimited drop-in pass ends after a month and never charges you again.

Where do I park?

We’re at 170 Serenoa Rd, Bay 3, in Santa Rosa Beach. Open it in Maps and text us if you get turned around.

Is it air conditioned?

Yes. It’s Florida. We’re not monsters.

Can I just walk in?

Please don’t. Buy a pass and reserve your spot online first. Classes fill up and we hate turning people away at the door.
