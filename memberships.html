What you get

Three ways to sweat.

Every membership covers all of it. We write our own programming, so it’s built for the people actually in the room.

01 · CrossFit

When in doubt, sweat it out

Relatively high intensity, constantly varied, one coached hour. Real coaching, moving well, and having fun doing it. Our slogan? Go HAMF. Hard as a Mo Fitter.

02 · Functional Bodybuilding

Get built, not burnt

Controlled intensity with a big focus on tempo, single arm and single leg work, and strategic rest. Moves you won’t usually see in a CrossFit class. Our slogan? Fill the sleeves.

03 · Open Gym

A playground for adults

Time to work on whatever you want outside of class. A coach is there, but not running a workout. Reserve your spot the same way you would for a class.

Good questions

Before you ask.

Do I need to be in shape first?

Nope. That’s what the class is for. Every movement scales, and your coach will set you up with the version that works for you today. When you’re brand new we take it slower and focus on moving well, not going fast.

How does the free trial work?

Fill out the short interest form and a coach will reach out to find a class that fits your schedule. We don’t do instant self-booking for trials because we want someone ready to meet you when you walk in.

What’s the difference between Unlimited and the Sessions Pack?

Unlimited is every class, every week, billed monthly. The Sessions Pack is 30 sessions you can use over 4 months, which works well if you split your time between 30A and somewhere else.

Can my family join?

Yes. Add each family member to an Unlimited membership for $150 a month. Spouses, kids, whoever.

I’m only here for a week.

Then you want a drop-in pass, not a membership. Single classes are $25 and a week of unlimited classes is $85. See drop-in passes.

What should I bring?

Shoes you can move in, a water bottle, and a good attitude. We have all the equipment.
