About us

A serious gym that refuses to take itself seriously.

We will happily talk your ear off about squat mechanics. We will also cheer embarrassingly loud when you row your first 500 meters. Both things are true and we see no conflict.

The story

Why this place exists.

Somewhere along the way, gyms got weird. Either nobody talks to you and you wander between machines, or everything is chalk dust and screaming and "no days off" until your shoulders file a complaint.

Momentum Fitness is our answer to both. It's a CrossFit gym in Santa Rosa Beach where the coaching is legitimately excellent, the standards are legitimately high, and absolutely nobody cares whether you showed up to set a personal record or just to shake off a long day. You'll get fit here. You'll also get high-fived by a stranger within about a week, and shortly after that they won't be a stranger.

Our whole mission fits in one sentence: get fit, make it the best hour of your day, stay safe, turn up the music, high five some people. We wrote it that way on purpose. If a decision doesn't serve that sentence, we don't do it.

Locals, snowbirds, vacationers, first-timers, and firebreathers: the door's open. Sundays we're closed, because even we go to the beach.
