About us

Your new gym home.

Momentum Fitness is a CrossFit gym in Santa Rosa Beach, a few minutes off 30A. We coach every class, we write our own programming, and we’d honestly rather you have fun than set a record.

Our mission, word for word
Get fit. Make it the best hour of your day. Stay safe. Turn up the music. High five some people.
We inspire people to live meaningful, happy, productive and connected lives.
